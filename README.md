# PSX Horror Props Free

**12 free PS1-style props for your survival horror game**, with moving parts and an inventory icon each: a padlock that snaps open, a fuse box that opens and throws its lever, a lantern that swings, and the keys, notes and healing herb every save room needs. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per prop, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Horror Props](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-props)** has 64 props and 22
animations: a floor safe whose dial spins and door swings open, a typewriter that types, an item
chest, a key cabinet, levers, valves and gauges, medical supplies, tapes and ammo, and a Unity
package. Same addon, same look: it installs over this one.

### What's inside

- **Keys and locks:** a skeleton key, a red keycard, a padlock that opens, a card reader.
- **Power:** a fuse box that opens and pulls its lever, and a cartridge fuse to put in it.
- **Light:** a flashlight and an oil lantern that swings.
- **Save room and supplies:** a handwritten note, an ink ribbon, a healing herb, a crowbar.
- **4 animations** on the moving parts.
- **12 inventory icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, rendered from the models.
- 8 to 406 triangles a prop, one 512x512 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours,
  real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per prop: box colliders on every part (the moving ones move with them), an
AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your flashlight falls on them, and a gallery.
Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# fuse_box: the prop in your level
fuse_box.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the props (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.
